NFR-USE-1 & The registration and OTP flow shall be completable within 5 minutes. & \textbf{Complete (user-tested).} The completion-time target is accepted based on user-performed testing; registration/OTP flows remain implemented. \\ \hline
NFR-USE-2 & Responsive support from 320 px to 1920 px should be validated with browser testing and additional breakpoints. & \textbf{Partial / Target (unverified).} A responsive viewport and some fluid widths exist, but only one media query is present and some pages have fixed minimum widths. \\ \hline
NFR-USE-3 & University, area, and subject browsing should preserve consistent navigation once all labeled navigation destinations are routed. & \textbf{Partial.} Browsing shares the forum shell and navigation, but several sidebar links point to unregistered routes or fallback views. \\ \hline
NFR-USE-4 & Primary actions should keep clear labels and shared controls, with accessibility testing for non-native controls. & \textbf{Partial.} Shared labeled buttons/inputs exist, but some controls are \texttt{div}-based and no usability/accessibility test evidence is present. \\ \hline
NFR-USE-5 & Usability at 320 px should be verified after fixed minimum-width layouts are addressed. & \textbf{Partial / Target (unverified).} A mobile viewport is declared, but some pages use 700 px/300 px minimum widths and no 320 px test exists. \\ \hline
NFR-USE-6 & Report and moderation actions should be reachable within 2 interactions when those actions are implemented. & \textbf{Planned (not implemented).} No report or moderation UI/routes exist. \\ \hline
