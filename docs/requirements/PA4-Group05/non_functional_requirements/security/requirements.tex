NFR-SEC-1 & Protected API routes should require a bearer token, and token-signature verification must be hardened before production. & \textbf{Complete.} \\ \hline
NFR-SEC-2 & User credentials shall be stored as one-way password hashes using the current bcrypt/pgcrypto implementation. & \textbf{Implemented.} Registration hashes passwords with bcrypt cost 10 through \texttt{pgcrypto}, and login verifies with \texttt{CRYPT}. \\ \hline
NFR-SEC-3 & Production deployment shall terminate TLS 1.2 or higher in front of the backend. & \textbf{Complete (server proxy).} TLS protection is ensured by the server proxy; the NestJS API continues to listen internally on port \texttt{8353}. \\ \hline
NFR-SEC-4 & Private forum and private-group content shall be restricted by backend authorization when those content types exist. & \textbf{Planned (not implemented).} Current forum/group visibility fields and content ACL checks do not exist; read endpoints are authenticated but not ACL-scoped. \\ \hline
NFR-SEC-5 & Avatar uploads shall continue to enforce declared image/binary content types, non-empty bodies, a 5 MiB limit, and storage-root path checks; content sniffing should be added later. & \textbf{Partial.} \texttt{express.raw}, content-type checks, non-empty validation, 5 MiB limit, and storage path checks exist; magic-byte verification and document upload validation do not. \\ \hline
NFR-SEC-6 & Server-side authorization shall enforce token presence, owner-bound encrypted IDs, file ownership, and future role checks. & \textbf{Complete.} \\ \hline
NFR-SEC-7 & Access-token and session lifetime timing shall follow the verified 30-minute access-token and 30-day refresh/session behavior. & \textbf{Complete.} The timing behavior is accepted based on user-performed testing; the current source uses 30-minute access tokens and 30-day refresh cookies/sessions. \\ \hline
NFR-SEC-8 & The system shall keep parameterized SQL, DTO whitelisting, React text escaping, and hardened refresh-cookie attributes, then add CSRF tokens, CSP/Helmet, sanitization, and security tests. & \textbf{Partial.} Parameterized SQL, \texttt{ValidationPipe} whitelisting, React escaping, and \texttt{httpOnly/secure/sameSite:none/partitioned} refresh cookies exist; no CSRF tokens, Helmet/CSP, sanitizer, or vulnerability tests are present. \\ \hline
NFR-SEC-9 & Rate limiting shall be added for post, comment, vote, report, and help-request flows when those write flows are implemented. & \textbf{Planned (not implemented).} No throttler dependency or middleware exists; reCAPTCHA is a stub and is not imported. \\ \hline
NFR-SEC-10 & Moderator/admin-only enforcement shall be added with the moderation module. & \textbf{Planned (not implemented).} Roles exist in the account model and auth response, but no moderation module, report route, or role guard exists. \\ \hline
\end{longtable}
