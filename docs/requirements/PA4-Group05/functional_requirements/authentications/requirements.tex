\subsubsection*{US-01: Register Account}
\par\medskip

\textbf{As a} university student, \textbf{I want to} register for a Studitopia account with my university email, \textbf{so that} I can access academic forums and learning features.
\par\medskip

\begingroup
\small
\renewcommand{\baselinestretch}{1.0}\selectfont
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{6pt}
\begin{longtable}{|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.20\textwidth}|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.52\textwidth}|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.09\textwidth}|>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.09\textwidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{FR} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Category} & \textbf{Status} \\ \hline
\endhead
\textbf{FR-01.1 (Registration Form)} & The system shall provide a registration form containing full name, username, university email, password, and confirm password fields. & UI, Frontend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.2 (Password Confirmation)} & The system shall require the user to enter the password twice and reject submission when the values do not match. & UI, Frontend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.3 (Password Visibility Toggle)} & The system shall allow the user to toggle password visibility for password-entry fields. & UI, Frontend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.4 (Registration API)} & The system shall submit registration data to the backend and create a pending or active user account. & Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.5 (Unique Identity Constraints)} & The system shall enforce unique usernames and unique university email addresses. & Backend, Database & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.6 (Email Verification in Progress)} & The system shall indicate that email verification is required after registration. & UI, Frontend & Partial \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.7 (Registered General Login)} & The system shall allow a registered general user to log in after successful verification. & Backend, Database & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.8 (University Student Role)} & The system shall assign new registered accounts the student role by default. & Backend, Database & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.9 (Default Profile from Registration Data)} & The system shall initialize the user profile from the information supplied during registration. & Backend, Database & Partial \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.10 (Registration Feedback)} & The system shall display success and error feedback for registration outcomes. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.11 (CAPTCHA on Registration)} & The system shall require CAPTCHA verification on the registration form. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.12 (Backend ReCaptcha Verification)} & The system shall validate the CAPTCHA token on the backend before creating the account. & Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.13 (Email OTP Verification)} & The system shall send and verify a one-time password for email ownership confirmation. & Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.14 (Duplicate Email Rejection)} & The system shall reject registration when the email is already in use. & Frontend, Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.15 (Duplicate Username Rejection)} & The system shall reject registration when the username is already in use. & Frontend, Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.16 (No Explicit Terms Checkbox)} & The system shall not require the user to select a separate terms-and-conditions checkbox during registration. & UI, Frontend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.17 (Excluded Username Reserved List)} & The system shall reject usernames reserved by the platform or excluded from public use. & Backend, Database & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.18 (Unique User ID)} & The system shall assign each user a unique identifier. & Backend, Database & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.19 (Email Format Validation)} & The system shall validate the university email format before account creation. & Frontend, Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.20 (Strong Password Validation)} & The system shall enforce the password policy during registration. & Frontend, Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.21 (Name, Date, and Gender Fields)} & The system shall collect full name, date of birth, and gender during registration. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.22 (Status Active)} & The system shall set the account status to active after successful registration and verification. & Backend, Database & Partial \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.23 (OTP Resend)} & The system shall allow the user to request a new verification OTP. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.24 (OTP Expiration)} & The system shall expire verification OTPs after the configured time limit. & Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.25 (OTP Max Attempts)} & The system shall limit the number of failed OTP verification attempts. & Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.26 (Resend Cooldown)} & The system shall enforce a cooldown period between OTP resend requests. & Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.27 (OTP Reuse Blocked)} & The system shall prevent a used OTP from being accepted again. & Backend, Database & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.28 (Welcome Email After Signup)} & The system shall send a welcome email after successful account creation. & Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.29 (OAuth/Google Login)} & The system shall allow users to register or log in through a Google account. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.30 (Cancel Verification)} & The system shall allow the user to cancel the email verification flow. & UI, Frontend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.31 (Register via Phone)} & The system shall allow users to register using a phone number. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.32 (Terms Checkbox)} & The system shall require an explicit terms-and-conditions checkbox during registration. & UI, Frontend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.33 (Parent Email for Under 16)} & The system shall require parent or guardian email confirmation for users under 16. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.34 (Agree to Terms)} & The system shall record that the user agreed to the terms and policies. & Backend, Database & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.35 (CAPTCHA)} & The system shall present a CAPTCHA challenge before submitting registration. & UI, Frontend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.36 (Auto-Login After Verification)} & The system shall log the user in automatically after successful email verification. & Frontend, Backend & Partial \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.37 (Default Profile from Provided Data)} & The system shall create the user profile using the provided name, username, and email. & Backend, Database & Partial \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.38 (Redirect to Dashboard)} & The system shall redirect the user to the dashboard or home page after successful registration. & UI, Frontend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.39 (Welcome Email)} & The system shall send a welcome email to the newly registered user. & Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.40 (Login by Email)} & The system shall allow a registered user to log in using their email address. & Frontend, Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.41 (Password Change Feature)} & The system shall allow the user to change their password from the account settings. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.42 (Hidden Password by Default)} & The system shall hide password characters by default in password fields. & UI, Frontend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.43 (Academic Information Fields)} & The system shall collect university and major information during registration. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.44 (Password Strength Indicator)} & The system shall display password strength or validation feedback while the user types. & UI, Frontend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-01.45 (Unverified Account Restrictions)} & The system shall prevent unverified accounts from accessing protected platform features. & Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\end{longtable}
\endgroup

\subsubsection*{US-07: OTP Validation}
\par\medskip

\textbf{As a} user, \textbf{I want to} receive and submit a one-time password, \textbf{so that} I can verify my identity securely.
\par\medskip

\begingroup
\small
\renewcommand{\baselinestretch}{1.0}\selectfont
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}

\textbf{FR-07.5 (Invalid OTP Handling)} & The system shall reject invalid, expired, or previously used OTPs and display an appropriate message. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Complete \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-07.6 (OTP Resend)} & The system shall allow the user to request a new OTP and invalidate the previous OTP. & UI, Frontend, Backend, Database & Complete \\ \hline
