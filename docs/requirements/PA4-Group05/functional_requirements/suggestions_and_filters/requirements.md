### Post Filtering

**As a** university student, **I want to** filter posts in the selected forum, **so that** I can narrow the feed to posts relevant to my context.

| FR | Requirement | Category | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **FR-13.3 (Post Filtering)** | The system shall support filtering posts by My Post, area, university, major, course, tags, and post type. | UI, Frontend, Backend | Not implemented |

### US-24: Receive Forum Recommendations

**As a** university student, **I want to** receive forum recommendations, **so that** I can discover discussions that matter to my courses and interests.

| FR | Requirement | Category | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **FR-24.1 (Relevant Recommendations)** | The system shall recommend forum posts that are relevant to the user's university, major, courses, and interests. | Backend | Not implemented |
| **FR-24.2 (Trending Recommendations)** | The system shall recommend trending posts within the user's academic context. | Backend | Not implemented |
| **FR-24.3 (Unsolved Recommendations)** | The system shall recommend unsolved questions where the user may be able to help. | Backend | Not implemented |
| **FR-24.4 (Dismiss Recommendations)** | The system shall allow users to dismiss irrelevant recommendations. | UI, Frontend, Backend | Not implemented |
| **FR-24.5 (Recommendation Refresh)** | The system shall refresh recommendations when the user's preferences or forum context change. | Frontend, Backend | Not implemented |
