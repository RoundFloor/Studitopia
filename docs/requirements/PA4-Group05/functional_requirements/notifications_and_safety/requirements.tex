\subsubsection*{US-38: Receive Notifications}
\par\medskip

\textbf{As a} user, \textbf{I want to} receive notifications about relevant activities, \textbf{so that} I do not miss important academic interactions.
\par\medskip

\hline
\textbf{FR} & \textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Category} & \textbf{Status} \\ \hline
\endhead
\textbf{FR-38.1 (Forum Notifications)} & The system shall notify a user about relevant forum activity, including comments, answers, votes, true-response marks, and mentions. & Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-38.2 (Social Notifications)} & The system shall notify a user about friend requests, messages, group invitations, and help requests. & Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-38.3 (Room Notifications)} & The system shall notify a user about optional room invitations when the user enables room notifications. & Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-38.4 (Notification Center)} & The system shall provide an in-app notification center with unread indicators. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-38.5 (Notification Preferences)} & The system shall allow users to configure which notification types they receive and whether audio alerts are enabled. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\textbf{FR-38.6 (Mark Notifications Read)} & The system shall allow users to mark notifications as read. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline
\end{longtable}

\par\medskip

\textbf{FR-39.2 (Privacy Settings)} & The system shall allow a user to control their privacy settings. & UI, Frontend, Backend & Not implemented \\ \hline

\subsubsection*{US-40: Report Inappropriate Behavior}
\par\medskip

\textbf{As a} user, \textbf{I want to} report inappropriate communication or behavior, \textbf{so that} the platform can maintain a safe learning environment.
\par\medskip
