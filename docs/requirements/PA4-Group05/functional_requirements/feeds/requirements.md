### US-13: View the List of Posts

**As a** university student, **I want to** view a list of posts within the selected forum, **so that** I can discover relevant discussions.

| FR | Requirement | Category | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **FR-13.1 (Post List Display)** | The system shall display a list of posts for the selected university, major, or course forum. | UI, Frontend, Backend | Complete |
| **FR-13.2 (Post Summary)** | The system shall show the post title, author, post type, timestamp, vote score, comment count, and solved status in the list. | UI, Frontend | Complete |
| **FR-13.4 (Post Sorting)** | The system shall support sorting posts by newest, top trending, most voted, and most discussed. | UI, Frontend, Backend | Complete |
| **FR-13.5 (Open Post)** | The system shall allow users to open a post from the list to view its full details. | UI, Frontend | Complete |
