## AI Features: AI-Based Learner Matching and Recommendations  
**Performed by:** [Huy] | **Reviewed by:** [Binh] | **Edited by:** [Name]

Our platform integrates an AI-powered recommendation system to help users find suitable study partners, study rooms, and study tables based on their learning goals, subject interests, schedules, study preferences or their prompt.
The AI system provides three main features:      
- Study Partner Matching: Recommends compatible learners based on shared subjects, learning goals, study habits, and schedules, helping users find suitable partners for individual or group study.
- Study Room Recommendations: Suggests appropriate study rooms based on users' preferences, such as room size, study environment, desired level of interaction  and inputed prompt.
- Study Table Recommendations: Helps users find suitable tables within a hall room based on table availability, group size, prompt allowing individuals or groups to choose an appropriate place to study.

These AI-powered features simplify the process of finding study partners and suitable learning spaces. By providing personalized recommendations, our platform enhances convenience, encourages collaboration, and helps users create a more effective and comfortable study experience.
