Studitopia is designed for students and independent learners who want to improve their learning experiences through a shared virtual environment.
- High school and university students: Prepare for examinations, complete assignments, and study with peers.     
- Independent learners: Learn new skills and participate in online learning communities.     
- Study groups and project teams: Collaborate on assignments, exchange resources, and conduct virtual meetings.     
- University communities: Access shared learning resources, participate in academic discussions, and connect with other students.
### Usage Environments: Web Browser
